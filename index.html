About Us

Allied School has a proud tradition of academic excellence and innovation. Our faculty is dedicated to student success and our campus offers state-of-the-art facilities.

Founded in 1985, we have grown to serve students from diverse backgrounds. Our curriculum is designed to prepare students for the future, focusing on critical thinking and real-world skills.

Admission Process

Our admission process is straightforward and student-friendly. Follow these steps to apply:

  1. Review the admission requirements and program options.
  2. Complete the online application form below.
  3. Upload required documents (transcript, ID, etc.).
  4. Pay the application fee using our secure system.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the admission requirements?

Applicants should have completed secondary education with a minimum GPA of X.Y. International students must provide proof of English proficiency.

When is the application deadline?

The deadline for the fall semester applications is June 1st. For the spring semester, the deadline is November 1st.

Yes, Allied School School welcomes international applicants. Please see our International Student page for more details.
